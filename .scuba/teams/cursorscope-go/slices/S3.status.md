## Definition of done
1. `classifyInvocation` returns one of exactly five categories — `mcp | cli | skill | subagent | tool` — with a name and detail across cursorscope's transcribed table. The five values are **External evidence**, read from the literal `category:` assignments in `src/attribution.js:174,183,193,226,234,242`, not from prose.
2. A `Profile` built from **SP1's real fixture** reports `skill_activation: present` with a **non-empty** `SkillName`, `Source: "hooks"`, and `Trigger: "inferred_file_read"`.
3. `attribution: present` with at least one `Attribution.Target` that **resolves to a `ToolCallEntry.ID` in the same profile** (R-SA-07 — the epic's primary success condition). Round 1 said "mapping at least one `tool_use_id` to that skill", which no field carried: `Attribution.Target` (`types.go:114-118`) is documented as a tool call ID and nothing in the contract produced one. SC adds `ToolCallEntry.ID` and the referential-integrity rule; S1b populates it; this DoD asserts against it.
4. The `Trigger` value comes from the **hook-inferred** vocabulary only — `inferred_file_read | inferred_shell_command | inferred_subagent` (**D6**, a Design decision of this epic), which is disjoint from Cursor's first-party enum `agent_read | manually_attached | skill_name_in_prompt` (**Specification**, ledger §A5.2:169). SC defines both sets; this slice may emit only the `inferred_*` one.
